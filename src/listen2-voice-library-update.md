## What changed

We have retired some of the downloadable voices in Listen2 after a review of the voice models we offer. The retired voices are older Piper voices. The library also has new voices, including updated versions of several voices you may know, so if one of yours is gone, take a look there.

## If you already downloaded a retired voice

Nothing changes for you. The voice stays on your device and keeps working. If it was your voice before the update, it is still your voice after it.

In the Voice Library, a retired voice carries a **Legacy** label, and the line under its name says "No longer offered". A short notice at the top of the Voice Library explains the change until you tap **Got It**.

A retired voice can no longer be downloaded. If you delete it, it will not come back, so think twice before deleting one you still use.

## The 1.5 voices

Several Piper voices have been retrained. A retrained voice carries a **1.5** label. It does not replace the older version of the same voice: the two sit side by side under the same name. If you have the older version, keep it as long as you like, and download the 1.5 version to compare.

## Languages

Listen2 still reads all 13 of its languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Hungarian, Turkish, Swedish, Russian, Greek, Hindi and Vietnamese.

Piper voices now cover nine of them. Greek, Turkish, Hindi and Vietnamese no longer have Piper voices. Supertonic voices read all 13 languages, including those four. Supertonic needs a device with more than 4 GB of memory, so on an iPhone or iPad with 4 GB or less, Greek, Turkish, Hindi and Vietnamese are read by Apple's own voices.
