You are on a plane. You have a two-hour flight and a 30-page report you need to get through before landing. You open your text-to-speech app and press play. Nothing happens. The app needs an internet connection to reach its voices.

This is the usual trade with text-to-speech apps. The voices that sound human run on someone else's servers, so your text is sent away to be read and the app stops working when the connection does: on a plane, in a subway, in the mountains, or in a building with thick walls. The voices built into iOS do work offline, but many apps can only offer the older, flatter ones.

You should not have to choose between a voice you can stand to listen to and an app that works everywhere.

## What to Look For in an Offline Text-to-Speech App

Here is what matters, whichever app you end up with:

- **Everything works offline.** Not "some voices" or "after a sync". Turn on airplane mode and try it before you rely on it.
- **The voices sound like people.** You will be listening for hours. A voice that is tiring after ten minutes is not a real option.
- **It reads the page as written.** A voice that skips a sentence or stumbles on a number is worse than no voice, because you do not know what you missed.
- **It opens your files.** PDFs, EPUB books, Word documents and web articles, without converting them first.
- **You can follow along.** Seeing each word highlighted as it is spoken makes it much easier to keep your place.
- **Your reading stays private.** If the voices run on the phone, your documents never have to leave it.

## It Reads What You Already Have

Listen2 opens PDFs, EPUB books, Word documents, plain text and web articles. Two-column papers are read in the right order, and books keep their chapters. Each word is highlighted as it is spoken, with every voice, so your eyes and your ears stay in the same place. If you look away, you can find your spot again at a glance.

You can also group documents into a collection and listen to them one after another, like a [personal podcast](/blog/turn-saved-articles-into-personal-podcast/).

## Make the Voice Read It Your Way

Most apps give you a speed slider and stop there. Listen2 has speed from 0.5x to 2.5x, and it goes further:

- **Voice Profile.** Each Listen2 voice has a Pace control for how fast it speaks and a Liveliness control for how much it rises and falls. Slower for a contract, livelier for a novel.
- **Pronunciation rules.** When a name comes out wrong, write it the way it sounds, such as SEER-shuh, and the voice says that from then on.
- **Words with two readings.** English has many words that are spelled one way and said two ways: read, lead, tear, record. The Listen2 voices say them the way the sentence means them.
- **Your own voice.** Read a short passage aloud and Listen2 makes a voice shaped by your recording, on your phone. It can then read any document to you.
