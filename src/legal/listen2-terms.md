## What Listen2 does

Listen2 reads documents aloud. It turns PDFs, EPUBs, web articles, text and other documents into speech, and highlights each word as it is read. The voices, the word highlighting and the other processing run on your device. The App goes online only for the reasons set out in the [Listen2 Privacy Policy](/legal/listen2-privacy/), such as downloading voices.

The App offers several kinds of voices:

- **Listen2 Voices**, made by Moonquake Media with our own speech model.
