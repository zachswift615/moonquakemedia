## Who we are

Moonquake Media ("we", "our" or "us") makes the Listen2 app for iPhone, iPad and Mac (the "App"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Listen2 was built to read your documents aloud without sending them anywhere. The voices, the word highlighting, text recognition for scanned pages, image descriptions and the voices you make all run on your device. Because of that, there is very little for this policy to cover, but the App does go online in a few specific situations, and each one is listed below.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create or import in Listen2 is stored on your device:

- Your documents: PDFs, EPUBs, Word files, DAISY books, web articles, text you paste, and the images inside them
- Reading progress, bookmarks and collections
- Settings, voice presets and pronunciation rules
- The voices you make, and the recordings they are made from
- Voices and other files the App has downloaded

We cannot see any of it. Reading a document aloud, timing the highlight to the voice, recognizing the text of a scanned page, describing figures, translating those descriptions and making a voice all happen on your device. None of that work is sent to a server.

## Voices you make

Listen2 can make a voice from a recording of a person reading. This section explains what the App creates, where it is kept and how to delete it. We treat voice recordings, and the voice data made from them, as sensitive personal information, including where the law calls them biometric information or biometric identifiers.

### What the App creates

When you make a voice, Listen2 creates and keeps these on your device:

- **The recording.** The reading you record in the App or the audio file you choose. The App keeps a prepared copy of up to 30 seconds of it, so the voice can be made again when the speech model is updated.
- **The voice sample.** The clearest 10 seconds or so of that recording, which the voice is built from.
- **Voice data.** Numbers that the speech model computes from the sample to describe the voice. They are stored so the voice does not have to be computed again every time it reads.
- **The voice's details.** The name you give it, the date it was made, and a record that consent was given.
- **The consent check.** Before it makes a voice, the App asks for the speaker's consent. Where the speaker says a consent sentence aloud, the App compares that sentence with the reading to check that the same person spoke both times. To do that it computes a speaker profile from each recording on your device. The consent recording and the result of the check are kept with the voice on your device. Where the App checks that the right words were said, it uses Apple's speech recognition only when that recognition can run on the device.

The microphone is used only while you record for a voice.

### Where it is kept, and who can see it

All of it is stored in the App's own storage on your device. It is never sent to Moonquake Media or to any server, and we have no way to access it. We do not sell, share, rent or disclose it, and we do not use it for anything except making the voice, checking consent and reading your documents in that voice in the App.

Your voices are not included in iCloud Sync, and they are not included in the backup file the App exports.

Your device's own backup may include them, as it includes other app data. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, the voice sample, the voice data and the voice's details can be part of that backup, held by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) or on your computer. The longer recording the App keeps is excluded from device backups.

### How long it is kept, and how to delete it

A voice and everything the App keeps for it stays on your device until you delete the voice or delete the App.

- **Delete one voice:** in the Voice Library, under Your Listen2 Voices, swipe left on the voice and tap Delete. This removes the voice, its recordings, its voice data and its consent record from your device.
- **Delete everything:** delete the App. This removes every voice and every recording the App stored.

An audio file you chose to make a voice from stays wherever you keep it; the App does not move or delete it. While you record, the App uses a temporary working file, which your next recording replaces and which iOS clears from time to time. Copies in an existing device backup remain until that backup is replaced or deleted.

### If you make a voice of someone else

Only make a voice of a person who has agreed and who takes part in the consent step themselves. The recording and the voice stay on the device it was made on. If the person asks you to, delete the voice as described above. The [Terms of Use](/legal/listen2-terms/) set out the rules for making voices, including the rules for children's voices.

## When Listen2 connects to the internet

### Downloading voices and other files

Some voices and models are not included in the App and are downloaded the first time you choose them: voice engines and their voices, the smaller Listen2 speech package for some devices, the models that time the word highlight for each language, pronunciation data, the rules that read numbers, dates and abbreviations correctly in each language, and the image description model.

These files come from our own file host at assets.moonquakemedia.com (served through Cloudflare) and from our public release page on GitHub. The list of some downloadable voices is read from GitHub. A download request asks for a specific file and contains no information about you or your documents. As with any web request, those services see your device's IP address and the file requested, and may keep routine server logs under their own privacy policies. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

### Playing a voice sample

When you play a sample of a voice in the Voice Library, a short audio file and a list of available samples may be fetched from moonquakemedia.com. Any sample of a voice you make is made on your device.

### Importing a web page or a link

When you import a link, or share a web page to Listen2 from Safari or another app, Listen2 fetches that page from the website you chose. If the page does not come through cleanly, Listen2 may also fetch another version of the same article that the page itself points to, and it downloads the article's images so they are saved with it. A public Google Drive link is fetched from Google. These sites see an ordinary request from your device, in the same way they would if you opened the page in a browser. The text and images are then stored on your device.

### Purchases and the free trial

Purchases, restores and redeemed codes are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To work out when your free trial ends, Listen2 asks Apple for the date the App was first downloaded on your Apple Account and also saves that date in your device's Keychain. A completed purchase is also remembered in the Keychain. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync keeps your library the same on all of your devices. On a new installation, Listen2 turns it on when iCloud Drive is available on your device. If you already had documents in Listen2 when sync arrived, the App asks you first. You can turn it on or off at any time in Listen2's Settings, under iCloud & Backup, iCloud Sync.

When it is on, these are stored in Listen2's folder in your own iCloud Drive:

- Your documents, as files, or as text for text you pasted, web articles and clipboard items
- Each document's title, the link it came from, your reading position and a short snippet of the text where you stopped
- Bookmarks, collections and collection artwork

Settings, voice presets, pronunciation rules, downloaded voices and the voices you make, with their recordings, are not synced.

Your iCloud data is held by Apple in your iCloud account, under Apple's terms and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We cannot access it. Turning sync off stops Listen2 from syncing, but it does not delete what is already in iCloud. To delete it, open your device's Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your storage, choose Listen2 and delete its data.

### Backup files you export

Export Backup, in Listen2's Settings under iCloud & Backup, makes a single file with what you choose to include, such as your settings, voice presets, pronunciation rules, the list of voices you have downloaded, collections, artwork and documents. It does not contain the voices you make or their recordings. The file goes wherever you choose to save or send it. We do not receive it unless you send it to us.

If iCloud Backup is on, Listen2's data may also be included in your device's backups, as described under "Voices you make".

### Apple features

Some features belong to your device's operating system: Apple voices and their downloads, the languages used to translate image descriptions, speech recognition, and Siri. Your device handles those under Apple's Privacy Policy.

### Links you tap

Links to the user guide, the support page, the open-source licences or this policy open in your browser. Our website is covered by the [Moonquake Media website privacy policy](/privacy.html).

## What we do not collect

| Data | Collected? |
|------|-----------|
| Name, email or other contact details | No (only if you email us) |
| Your documents or their contents | No |
| Voice recordings, voice samples or voice data | No |
| What you read, or for how long | No |
| Location | No |
| Device or advertising identifiers | No |
| Analytics or usage tracking | No |
| Crash reports | Only through Apple, if you allow it (see below) |

Listen2 contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code.

### Crash and performance reports

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in your device's Analytics settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and performance figures for Listen2. They contain no personal information and nothing about your documents, and you can turn the setting off at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements. Listen2 also reads the daily performance summaries iOS provides and writes them to your device's own log. They are not sent to us.

### Playback diagnostics

Listen2 keeps a small log of playback events on your device, so that a problem such as "it would not play in the car" can be looked into. It records events such as play, pause, interruptions and audio route changes, their timings, the names of your audio devices (for example, a car's or headphones' Bluetooth name), your device model, your system version and the Listen2 version. Where it notes a sentence, it records only a short fingerprint and the sentence's length, not the text. It does not record what you are reading.

The log never leaves your device on its own. Export Playback Diagnostics, in Listen2's Settings under About, makes a copy that you can look at and share. We receive it only if you choose to send it to us, and we use it only to look into your problem. Deleting the App deletes the log.

## Emailing support

If you email us from the App, the message is written in your own mail app and includes your device model, system version and Listen2 version so we can help. You can see and edit all of it before you send it. We use your email, and any diagnostics file you attach, only to reply to you, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

## Children's privacy

Listen2 does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age. A voice made from a child's recording stays on the device it was made on, like every other voice. The Terms of Use require a parent or guardian to make or approve any such voice. Parents should also know that Listen2 reads aloud whatever text is imported into it.

## Keeping your data safe

Because your data is stored on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. A strong passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and up-to-date software are the best protection. If you use iCloud, Apple's Advanced Data Protection adds end-to-end encryption to iCloud Drive and iCloud Backup.

## Your choices

- **See your data:** everything you have imported, and every voice you have made, is in the App.
- **Delete your data:** delete documents or voices in the App, or delete the App to remove everything it stored on the device. If you use iCloud Sync, turn it off and delete Listen2's data in your iCloud settings.
- **Stay offline:** once the voices you want are on your device, Listen2 reads with no connection at all.

## Privacy rights where you live

We do not collect, sell or share personal information, so under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation there is nothing we hold to disclose, correct or delete. This includes voice recordings and voice data: they stay on your device, under your control, and you can delete them at any time as described above. The one exception is an email you choose to send us, with anything you attach to it. For that, Moonquake Media is the data controller, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you, and you may ask us at any time to see or delete it.
