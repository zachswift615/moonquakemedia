## Who we are

Moonquake Media ("we", "our" or "us") makes the Listen2 app for iPhone and iPad (the "App"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Listen2 was built to read your documents aloud without sending them anywhere. The voices, the word highlighting, text recognition for scanned pages and image descriptions all run on your device. Because of that, there is very little for this policy to cover, but the App does go online in a few specific situations, and each one is listed below.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create or import in Listen2 is stored on your device:

- Your documents: PDFs, EPUBs, Word files, DAISY books, web articles, text you paste, and the images inside them
- Reading progress, bookmarks and collections
- Settings, voice presets and pronunciation rules
- Voices and other files the App has downloaded

We cannot see any of it. Reading a document aloud, timing the highlight to the voice, recognizing the text of a scanned page, describing figures and translating those descriptions all happen on your device. None of that work is sent to a server.

## When Listen2 connects to the internet

### Downloading voices and other files

Some voices and models are not included in the App and are downloaded the first time you choose them: voice engines and their voices, the models that time the word highlight for each language, the rules that read numbers, dates and abbreviations correctly in each language, and the image description model.

These files come from our own file host at assets.moonquakemedia.com (served through Cloudflare) and from our public release page on GitHub. The list of available voices is read from GitHub. A download request asks for a specific file and contains no information about you or your documents. As with any web request, those services see your device's IP address and may keep routine server logs under their own privacy policies. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

### Playing a voice preview

When you play a sample in the Voice Library, a short audio file is streamed from moonquakemedia.com.

### Importing a web page or a link

When you import a link, or share a web page to Listen2 from Safari or another app, Listen2 fetches that page from the website you chose. If the page does not come through cleanly, Listen2 may also fetch another version of the same article that the page itself points to, and it downloads the article's images so they are saved with it. A public Google Drive link is fetched from Google. These sites see an ordinary request from your device, in the same way they would if you opened the page in a browser. The text and images are then stored on your device.

### Purchases and the free trial

Purchases, restores and redeemed codes are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To work out when your free trial ends, Listen2 asks Apple for the date the App was first downloaded on your Apple Account and also saves that date in your device's Keychain. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### iCloud

iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your library and settings are stored in your own iCloud account so they can appear on your other devices. If iCloud Backup is on, Listen2's data may also be included in your device's backups, and a backup file you export goes wherever you choose to save it. Your iCloud data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it.

### Apple features

Some features belong to iOS itself: downloading Apple voices, downloading the languages used to translate image descriptions, and Siri. iOS handles those under Apple's Privacy Policy.

### Links you tap

Links to the user guide, the support page or this policy open in your browser. Our website is covered by the [Moonquake Media website privacy policy](/privacy.html).

## What we do not collect

| Data | Collected? |
|------|-----------|
| Name, email or other contact details | No (only if you email us) |
| Your documents or their contents | No |
| What you read, or for how long | No |
| Location | No |
| Device or advertising identifiers | No |
| Analytics or usage tracking | No |
| Crash reports | Only through Apple, if you allow it (see below) |

Listen2 contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code.

### Crash and performance reports

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in your device's Analytics settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and performance figures for Listen2. They contain no personal information and nothing about your documents, and you can turn the setting off at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements. Listen2 also reads the daily performance summaries iOS provides and writes them to your device's own log. They are not sent to us.

## Emailing support

If you email us from the App, the message is written in your own mail app and includes your device model, iOS version and Listen2 version so we can help. You can see and edit all of it before you send it. We use your email only to reply to you, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

## Children's privacy

Listen2 does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Parents should know that Listen2 reads aloud whatever text is imported into it.

## Keeping your data safe

Because your data is stored on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. A strong passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and up-to-date software are the best protection.

## Your choices

- **See your data:** everything you have imported is in the App.
- **Delete your data:** delete documents in the App, or delete the App to remove everything it stored on the device. If you use iCloud Sync, turn it off and delete Listen2's data in your iCloud settings.
- **Stay offline:** once the voices you want are on your device, Listen2 reads with no connection at all.

## California and European privacy rights

We do not collect, sell or share personal information, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU General Data Protection Regulation. The one exception is an email you choose to send us. For that, Moonquake Media is the data controller, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you, and you may ask us at any time to see or delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If the App starts handling information differently, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page before the change ships.
